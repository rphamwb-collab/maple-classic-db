What I learned:

**A brutal October drawdown** - NBIS slid from roughly $250 early in the month to $159.17 at the Oct 22 close, down 7.23% that day ([StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nbis/)). The worst single hit came Oct 16, an 11.99% drop to $165.47 and the sixth decline in eight sessions, as the whole neocloud group sold off ([GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9128275/nebius-group-nbis-shares-plunge-nearly-12-amid-sector-selloff-on-october-16-2026)). The backdrop was the Oct 14 data center selloff, when [r/StockMarket](https://www.reddit.com/r/StockMarket/comments/1x62h95/dell_stock_plunges_11_leading_data_center_selloff/) tracked Dell's 11% plunge on "enterprise AI timing" fears.

**Debt is the bear story** - On Oct 22 Nebius took a $3.5B, 364-day secured loan from Citi, and the stock fell about 6% anyway ([24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/10/22/nebius-declines-6-despite-fresh-citi-financing-coreweave-drops-5-cloudflare-edges-higher/)). [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/nebius-citi-bridge-loan-gpu-prices) reports the company already carries about $10B of convertible debt, and the COO points to $40B in long-term contracts as future collateral. Critics flag refinancing risk from the short tenor and customer concentration ([Invezz](https://invezz.com/news/2026/10/22/nebius-stock-falls-as-3-5b-loan-raises-ai-infrastructure-debt-concerns/)). A widely followed Stocktwits account summed up the mood: "Credit is really starting to put pressure on the AI trade" ([cmgventure](https://stocktwits.com/cmgventure/message/667231870)).

**Analysts split, but most still say buy** - Piper Sandler cut its target from $237 to $185 and stayed Neutral on Oct 21 ([MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/analyst-nebius-group-nasdaq-nbis-price-target-cut-to-18500-by-analysts-at-piper-sandler-2026-10-21/)). Bank of America reiterated Buy at $310 the next day, and the consensus target sits near $284 ([StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nbis/forecast/)). Michael Burry reportedly moved from a straight short to June puts ([Timothy Sykes](https://www.timothysykes.com/news/nebius-group-nv-nbis-news-2026_10_22/)).

**Retail is buying the dip** - YouTube creators framed the drop as a setup. On Stocktwits, posts like "this stock has a history of recovering very quickly after drops" ([vulcaninvestor](https://stocktwits.com/vulcaninvestor/message/667228113)) dominated. [TheTeslaGuy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32EcA9mEWrA) flagged $130 as the next level to watch if the slide continues.

**Deal flow kept coming** - Earlier in the window, Nebius signed a 50 MW, 12-year capacity deal with AIB and bought AI startup Inferize, which sparked an Oct 19 bounce that commentators tied partly to WallStreetBets momentum ([Timothy Sykes](https://www.timothysykes.com/news/nebius-group-nv-nbis-news-2026_10_19/)). [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/why-nebius-group-drawing-both-130208786.html) noted WSB bulls also focus on the company's ClickHouse stake.

**Earnings are next** - Q3 results are not out yet. MarketBeat estimates Nov 10, though the company has not confirmed a date ([Equibles](https://equibles.com/research/nebius-group-nbis-q3-2026-earnings-preview-and-forecast)). Consensus revenue is about $905M against 2026 guidance of $3.0B-$3.4B.

KEY PATTERNS from the research:
1. Sector, not company news, drove most of the drop - NBIS moved with CoreWeave and the Oct 14 data center selloff.
2. Debt-funded growth is the central bear argument - the short-dated Citi loan sharpened it rather than calming it.
3. Wall Street targets sit far above the price - most analysts still rate it Buy, with one notable cut to $185.
4. Retail sentiment stays bullish - creators and commenters frame the slide as a dip to buy.
5. Earnings around Nov 10 are the next real test - watch revenue against the $3.0B-$3.4B guide and any financing update.
